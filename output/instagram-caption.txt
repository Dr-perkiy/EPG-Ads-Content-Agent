There may be a second Google listing with your name on it, and you never created it.

It happens after a move, a rebrand, or a name change. Google builds a listing from old data and it sits there collecting a few of your reviews.

Here is how to handle it:

1. Search your business name in Google Maps, then your phone number, then your old address. Duplicates usually show up lower with no photos.

2. Understand the damage. Reviews, clicks, and direction requests get split between two listings, so neither one looks strong to Google.

3. Claim the duplicate, then request a merge through Google Business Profile support. Do not delete it, or the reviews on it disappear with it.

Most Google Business Profiles have messaging turned off, and the owner has no idea.

1. Turn messaging on. In Google Search or the Google Business Profile app, enable chat. A message button appears on your listing next to call and directions.

2. Set an away reply. One line saying when you will respond, plus a question that keeps them typing.

3. Reply fast. Google can disable messaging for slow responders, so switch on notifications and answer within minutes during business hours.

Some people will never call a stranger. They will text. Give them the option.

Stuffing keywords into your Google business name works, right up until your listing vanishes.

1. Your profile name has to match your real world signage. Sign, truck, invoice, window. If a reviewer checks and it does not match, that is a suspension.

2. Competitors report stuffed names in under a minute, and Google's automated checks flag city and service words too. You are hiding from two systems, not one.

3. A hard suspension takes your reviews with it. Appeals can run for weeks and you need your license, a utility bill, and photos of your signage to get back in. That is a bad trade for a temporary bump.

Put the keywords in your services, description, and website instead. Same lift, no risk.

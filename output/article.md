# How to Use the Google Business Profile Products Section as a Tampa Service Business

If you run a service business in Tampa or Brandon, you have probably opened the Google Business Profile products section, seen the word products, and closed it again. You do not sell anything on a shelf. So you moved on.

That is the mistake. Google does not verify that you stock physical goods. The products section is really a card builder, and service businesses can fill it with services. Most of your competitors have not done it, which is exactly why it is worth an afternoon.

Why an empty tab costs you

When someone finds you in the map pack and taps your name, they land on your profile. Across the top sit tabs: overview, reviews, photos, products, services. If products is empty, there is nothing to hold them. They go back to the results and open the next listing.

A filled products tab does two jobs. It keeps a real person reading, and it adds text and images tied to your listing that Google can associate with your business.

Point one: list every service as a product

Go to your profile, open the edit menu, and find products. Add one entry per service you actually want more of.

An HVAC company in Riverview might add seasonal tune up, duct cleaning, emergency repair and full system replacement. A law firm in Brandon might add estate planning consultation, will package, probate representation. A salon might list cut and color, balayage, keratin treatment.

Do not dump your entire capability list in there. Pick the work that pays well and that you want the phone to ring for. Five to twelve entries is plenty for most local businesses.

Point two: use all three fields

Each entry holds three things, and all three matter.

The photo. Use a real picture from a real job. Your own truck, your own chair, your own workshop. Stock photos read as stock photos, and they do nothing for trust.

The price. You get a single price or a range. Many owners freeze here because every job is different. Use a range with a starting figure instead. Something like a starting price for a standard visit tells a shopper you are in their budget without committing you to a number you cannot hold. A range beats a blank field every time, because the person comparing you to two other listings will pick the one that gave them information.

The description. You get roughly a thousand characters. That is far more room than the short business description Google gives you. Write plainly about what the job includes, how long it takes, and what the customer should expect.

Point three: you gain text tied to your profile

Your Google Business Profile is a small container. The main description fills up quickly. Categories are just labels you pick from a list. Attributes are checkboxes. There is not much room to say what you actually do.

Product descriptions change that. Every entry you write adds more relevant text attached to your listing. Write the way a customer would ask for the service. If people call it AC tune up, write AC tune up, not preventative climate maintenance. Mention the area you serve once, naturally, like Valrico or Plant City. Do not repeat the city six times. That reads as spam to a human, and humans are who you are trying to convince.

How long this takes

Plan on two to three hours if you already have photos on your phone. Write your entries in a notes app first, then paste them in. Once they are live, review them twice a year, or whenever prices move.

FAQ

Can a service business really use the products section?
Yes. Google does not restrict it to physical goods. Service businesses across Tampa Bay use it for repairs, consultations, packages and memberships.

Do I have to show prices?
No, you can leave price blank, but a starting price or a range performs better with people who are comparing listings.

Is this the same as the services section?
No. Services is a simpler list tied to your categories. Products give you a photo, a price and a longer description, so use both.

Will this alone put me in the top 3?
On its own, no. It helps, and it is one of the easier items to fix. Ranking usually comes down to eight or ten things working together: categories, reviews and replies, citations, service area, photo activity and your website.

What if I have several locations?
Fill the products section for each profile separately, and adjust the descriptions for the neighborhood that location serves.

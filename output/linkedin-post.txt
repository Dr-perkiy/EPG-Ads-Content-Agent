Keyword stuffing a Google Business Profile name is still one of the most common shortcuts I see in Tampa Bay. It ranks, for a while.

Three things owners underestimate: your listed name must match your real world signage, competitors and Google's automated checks both flag stuffed names, and a hard suspension takes your reviews offline while you appeal.

Appeals need documents: business license, utility bill, photos of your signage. That process can run weeks during your busiest season.

The keywords still belong in your profile, just in the services, categories, and description where Google wants them.

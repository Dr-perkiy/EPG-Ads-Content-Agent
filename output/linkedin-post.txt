Most local business owners think page one is the goal. On a phone, page one is mostly the map pack.

The map pack is the block of three business listings shown under the map at the top of a local search. It is powered by your Google Business Profile.

Nearly all local clicks, calls, and direction requests go to those three listings, because they come with a call button before anyone scrolls.

That is why position seven in the regular results does so little. By the time a searcher reaches it, they have already picked someone.

Check it yourself: search your main service plus your city on your phone and count the scrolls to your name.

Most local ranking problems are not effort problems. They are eligibility problems.

Three settings decide whether Google will rank you at all: your primary category, your verification status, and whether your profile has been flagged or suspended.

A category that is too broad puts you in a race you cannot win. Incomplete verification limits how much Google trusts the listing. A quiet suspension removes you from Maps entirely, and the notice usually lands in spam.

Check all three from your dashboard this week. It takes about ten minutes.

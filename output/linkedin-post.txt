The business with worse reviews is beating you in the map pack. Here is why.

Proximity. Google ranks by distance from the searcher. A closer competitor wins that search regardless of star count.

Primary category. This decides which searches you show up in. The wrong category loses to a lower rated business with the right one.

Completeness. Hours, services, attributes, photos, and answered questions all signal an active business. A neglected five star profile loses to a fully built one.

Fix those three and most review gaps stop mattering.

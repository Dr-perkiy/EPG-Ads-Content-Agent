Service area businesses get this setting backwards more than any other field on a Google Business Profile.

List the cities you genuinely serve, not an entire state you would never drive to.

A wide radius does not expand reach. It spreads your relevance thin, so you slip in the towns that actually pay the bills.

Hide your address only if customers never visit you. If they do, a public address is a proximity signal worth keeping.

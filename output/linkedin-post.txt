Three free fixes for your Google Business Profile. Do them today.

1. Set the right primary category. Most owners choose something too generic, then never appear for the searches that matter. Be specific.

2. Fill in every field. Hours, service areas, services, attributes, products, photos. Google shows complete profiles more often than incomplete ones.

3. Post every week. One update with a photo is enough. Active profiles get more visibility.

That covers three of the eight to ten factors that usually decide the map pack. Reviews, citations, and your website pages handle the rest.

Most local businesses I audit in Tampa and Brandon have a duplicate Google listing they never created.

Google builds them from old data after a move, a rebrand, or a name change. The duplicate then splits your reviews and ranking signals across two profiles.

Fix it in three steps: search your name, phone number, and old address in Google Maps. Confirm which listing you manage. Claim the duplicate and request a merge through Google Business Profile support instead of deleting it, so the reviews carry over.

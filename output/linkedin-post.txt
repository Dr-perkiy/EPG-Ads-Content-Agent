You do not need to learn SEO to know whether your SEO provider is working. You need three questions.

1. What is my primary Google Business Profile category? It controls which searches you show up for, and anyone managing your profile knows it without looking.

2. How many local citations did you build or fix this month? Ask for the directories by name, with links.

3. Show me my current map pack position for my main keyword, measured from my service area. Position, not impressions.

A provider doing the work answers all three in minutes. A provider coasting on your retainer needs a week to build a slide about it.

Citations are the free local ranking factor most business owners in Tampa and Brandon have never looked at.

A citation is any online mention of your business name, address, and phone: Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing Places, chamber pages, industry directories.

Google compares those mentions. When they match, it gains confidence in you. When one old phone number or a dropped suite number is still live somewhere, that confidence drops and so does your map position.

Start here: search your phone number in quotes, list every site that appears, then correct anything that does not match your Google Business Profile exactly.

Most service businesses in Tampa leave the products section of their Google Business Profile completely empty.

Google does not require physical goods. List each service as its own entry: repairs, consultations, packages, memberships.

Every entry carries a photo, a price range and a description, so you gain more relevant text attached to your listing.

That text is real estate you cannot get anywhere else on the profile.

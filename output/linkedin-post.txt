Citations are the free local ranking factor most business owners have never heard of.

A citation is any mention of your business name, address, and phone across the web: Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing Places, directories, chamber pages. No link required for it to count.

Google builds confidence when those details match your Google Business Profile everywhere it looks. It loses confidence when they do not.

The usual culprit is small: an old tracking number from a former marketing company, or a suite number you left behind. It sits there and contradicts you.

Search your phone number in quotes, list what comes up, and correct the wrong ones this week.

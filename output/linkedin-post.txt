Reviews are not the reason your competitor is above you in the map pack.

Three things carry more weight:

Proximity. Google serves results close to the searcher. Someone in Wesley Chapel sees Wesley Chapel businesses first.

Primary category. Google matches the search to your primary category before it weighs your rating. The wrong category costs you the searches you want most.

Profile completeness. Hours, services, photos, answered questions. An active profile outranks a neglected one with a better star average.

All three are free to fix and sit inside your Google Business Profile.
